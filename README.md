# hi, i'm anupam

I build apps and write about them. Sometimes funny. Good at helping manage engineers, better with engineering managers.

**19 apps live on the App Store, 65 approved releases since December 2025**, made solo under my studio, [Alcor](https://getalcor.com/): research, design, build, App Review, launch. Now building [TradeShot](https://tradeshot.co), a calmer way to trade consciously: every move explained from evidence, and nothing places an order without your approval.

I was a reluctant engineer. I wanted to make films and play cricket, and my first app earned $2. I'm still here.

## shipping now

- **[Breakline](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6785873182)**: see how your body moves in your own clips, across seven sports. Measurements only, never a score, nothing uploaded. In testing: a 3D humanoid robot replays your surf pop-up or push-up.
- **[Wearover](https://getalcor.com/wardrobe/)**: pick a photo of you and a look. The clothes change; your face stays exactly as it is. 3.0 is in App Review.
- **[Screen Room](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6829207167)**: talk about a film without spoilers. The ending stays hidden until you ask.
- **[Orbits](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759101340)**: plan a trip or a meetup by finding common ground. 12 releases and counting.
- **[Rifflet](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6767064984)**: turn a music idea into a lesson anyone can play, kids included.
- **[First Contact Duet](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6818777737)**: two phones, one friendly visitor you greet with hand signs and chords.
- **[Machiss](https://github.com/anupamchugh/match-box)**: a private, local Mac companion for thinking through conversations. It made it to [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/mathis-s).
- **[MacItWork](https://macit.work/)**: send approved work from one Mac to another, pick the least busy one, and get a signed receipt back.
- **Glint**: a market desk that shows where every number came from. Its agents can draft a trade; none can place one without my approval of that exact order.

Every Apple Intelligence feature I ship answers from what the app actually measured, and Swift checks the answer before you see it. The model talks; the code decides.

## in the open

- **[iowncode](https://github.com/anupamchugh/iowncode)**: years of iOS, SwiftUI, Core ML, Vision and AR experiments. 900+ stars. More drawer of sparks than museum.
- **[Deckloom](https://github.com/anupamchugh/Deckloom)**: a SwiftUI card stack you can swipe, fan and open full screen.
- **[Pastport](https://github.com/anupamchugh/pastport)**: on-device Safari history intelligence for macOS. Your browser knows where you've been; this helps you read it.
